## 4 Requirements

OF2-R-201: The fan shall deliver strong airflow at the highest speed. (Airflow output, variable). Limit ≥ 1.8 m³/min at speed 3. Acceptance criterion: Mean − 3σ ≥ 1.8 m³/min.

OF2-R-202 (Noise level; variable). The fan shall run quietly at the lowest speed. Samples are accepted when All samples ≤ 42 dB(A); the design limit itself is ≤ 42 dB(A) at speed 1, 1 m.

Oscillation angle — OF2-R-203, Variable type. Verification succeeds if All samples within 75° to 85°. The head shall oscillate across a wide angle. Target: 80° ± 5°.

OF2-R-204: The motor shall stop within 2 s when the fan tilts beyond 15°. (Tip-over switch, attribute). Limit N/A. Acceptance criterion: 0 failures in n samples.

OF2-R-205 (Button actuation force; variable). The control buttons shall need a light press. Samples are accepted when All samples within 2 N to 5 N; the design limit itself is 2 N to 5 N.

Remote control range — OF2-R-206, Attribute type. Verification succeeds if 0 failures in n samples. The remote shall operate the fan at 6 m in line of sight. Target: N/A.

OF2-R-207: The test finger shall not touch moving parts through the guard. (Guard finger probe, attribute). Limit N/A. Acceptance criterion: 0 failures in n samples.
